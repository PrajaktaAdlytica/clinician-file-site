Governance / Research-stage concept

Permission travels
with the record.

A synthetic preview cannot establish healthcare compliance, lawful source access, or approval authority. The proposed operating model makes these dependencies explicit.

Permission is part of the product

When the answer is uncertain,
the record should say so.

No automated approval authority. No invented portal access. Every stage needs an identified reviewer and a source-linked audit trail.

Consent and authority

Provider consent, permission to submit on behalf of clinicians, and an authorized data-access route are prerequisites.

Source standards and freshness

Primary-source verification requirements, stale records, state variation, and exception criteria must be scoped.

Terms and oversight

Portal terms, licensing-board access, delegated-credentialing oversight, security review, and human approval govern any pilot.

A deliberate boundary

The MVP is a read-only, synthetic demonstration. No PHI, live integrations, outbound messages, patient contact, automated determinations, or writes to external systems.

Human review before action

Every source observation needs an identified verifier and an authorized access route. Missing consent, stale evidence, and conflicts remain reviewable exceptions. A local handoff is not a payer approval.

Scope before a live build

Confirm portal terms, licensing-board access, state variation, delegated oversight, security/data-access requirements, retention, and the authority to submit for clinicians.

What the preview does

Reads a local synthetic fixture, supports reversible in-memory review actions, and exports clearly labeled sample audit events. No patient data, live source access, outbound messages, external writes, analytics, or payments.
