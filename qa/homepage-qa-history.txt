## Capture method

Storyboard captures use the `?capture=1` review setting, which disables smooth anchor travel and entrance transition offsets so the frames show settled compositions. It does not replace content, hide controls, remove disclosures, or disable the scroll assembly. Interactive tests also exercise the actual controls. The normal user preview omits this setting.
