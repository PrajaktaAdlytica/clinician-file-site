The work it owns

Credential Proof

Enrollment and credentialing teams can inspect renewal obligations in context, with the original evidence and assigned reviewer nearby.

Inspect the related demo workflow
Inputs
Source-linked expiry dates; review cadence; state-specific requirements; assigned owner.
Workflow
Review the expiry horizon → inspect the evidence behind it → assign a human recheck before the agreed deadline.
Permission and exceptions
Cadence is sponsor-defined. A countdown does not establish that a credential is valid, renewed, or approved.
Proposed output and evidence
Review calendar and renewal queue. The demo uses a 31 December expiry and 72 days from its 20 October anchor.

Before a pilot

Is Credential Proof available today?

No. This is a proposed module and a synthetic concept preview. No adoption, live integration, or efficacy is established.

What would need to be agreed first?

Written scope, source and export rights, consent, a named operations owner, reviewer authority, baseline, security/data-access review, and the pilot readiness gates.

How would the commercial model work?

The willingness-to-pay test is $500 per 100 actively managed providers per month plus $4,000 implementation. This is hypothetical, not an active offer or separate module price.
